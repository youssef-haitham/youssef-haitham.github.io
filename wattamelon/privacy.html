Wattamelon: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Wattamelon is a game by YH Games for Fun, an individual developer in Egypt ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game keeps about you, why, and for how long. Questions: yhgamesforfun@gmail.com.

In short

  • You play without an account. Your levels, coins, boosters, lives and settings are kept on your device.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, a copy of your game is saved to that account, so you can bring it back on a new phone. This is optional.
  • When you buy something, the game makes an anonymous account on our server, so we can check the purchase with Google Play or the App Store and give it to you once. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a reward. They are shown by Google AdMob, muted, and limited to content suitable for everyone.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting. We never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account in the game at any time: SETTINGS → DELETE ACCOUNT.

1. What we keep, and why

WhatWhy
Your player ID: a random code made the first time you buy something or sign in. It is your account. To know which purchases are yours.
Your Google or Apple account, only if you choose to sign in with it: the email address that the company shares with us (Apple may give a private relay address instead), and its ID for your account. To save your game to that account and give it back to you when you sign in on another device.
Your saved game, only if you sign in: your level, stars, coins, boosters, lives, daily gift step, starter pack and the purchases already given to you. To bring your game back on another device (SETTINGS → RESTORE DATA).
Your purchases: the store (Google Play or the App Store), the order number, the product, the date, and the refund date if there is one. To give you what you paid for, to deliver each purchase only once, and to take a purchase back if the store refunds it.
Technical data: your IP address and the short "user agent" text your device sends with every request to our server. Our server provider's request logs also record the approximate location (such as country and city) that its network works out from the IP address. Every online service receives these. They are kept for security and to fix problems. We do not use them to find out where you are.

We get the details of a purchase from Google or Apple when they confirm it. We never see your card or bank details: Google and Apple handle all payments.

Kept on your device: your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives, the daily gift), your settings, the keys that keep you signed in to your anonymous account, and the list of purchases already given to you. If you remove the game, these are removed with it.

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to help you.

2. Ads

Some rewards can be earned by watching a short video ad, only when you tap to watch one. The ads are shown by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location, and information about how you interact with the ad, as described in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, through Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in SETTINGS → AD PRIVACY.
  • The ads are muted and limited to content rated for everyone. Gambling, alcohol, dating and other sensitive categories are blocked.
  • You can reset or delete your device's advertising ID, or limit ad personalisation, in your phone's settings.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • The game does not read your contacts, photos or location, and does not use your camera or microphone.

4. Who handles the data for us

  • Our cloud server provider runs our server: the anonymous accounts, the purchase records and its logs. The database is stored in Europe. The provider works only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement, and it and the companies it works with may reach the data from other countries, such as the United States, under the European Union's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store sell the game's items and take the payments, under their own terms and privacy policies. Our server asks them to confirm each purchase.
  • Google AdMob shows the ads you choose to watch, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Google and Apple sign you in when you choose to sign in with them, each under its own privacy policy. They never give us your password.

We do not share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

DataHow long
Your player ID, your Google or Apple account details, your saved game, and the link between you and your purchasesUntil you delete your account.
The purchase records themselves (store, order number, product, dates), without your player ID Kept after you delete your account, so the same receipt can never be used twice. They no longer point to you.
Our server provider's request logs (IP address, user agent, approximate location)Up to 7 days, then deleted automatically.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: SETTINGS → DELETE ACCOUNT. It deletes your player ID, your sign-in details and your saved game on our server at once. Your game on the device stays. You can also ask us by email.
  • Ads: SETTINGS → AD PRIVACY (where it applies), and your phone's advertising settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to how it is used. Email us and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

Wattamelon is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All connections to our server are encrypted (HTTPS). Only the anonymous account that made a purchase can see its own records, and only our server can record a purchase.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
