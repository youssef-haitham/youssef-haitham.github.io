Wattamelon: Delete Your Account

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Wattamelon is a game by YH Games for Fun. You can delete your account and the data our server keeps with it at any time. Our Privacy Policy explains what that data is.

In the game (at once)

  1. Open Wattamelon and tap the gear (SETTINGS) on the home page.
  2. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT, then DELETE.

Your account is deleted from our server straight away. The game on your phone stays, so you can keep playing.

By email (within 30 days)

Write to yhgamesforfun@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Wattamelon account". So we can find your account, send one of these:

  • the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with, or
  • the order number of a purchase you made in the game (it is in the receipt email from Google Play or the App Store).

We answer within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DeletedKept
Your player ID, your Google or Apple sign-in details, your saved game, and the link between you and your purchases. The purchase records themselves (store, order number, product, dates), without your player ID, so the same receipt can never be used twice. They no longer point to you. Our server's request logs are deleted by themselves within 7 days.

If you never bought anything and never signed in, our server has no account for you: your game lives only on your phone, and removing the game removes it.
